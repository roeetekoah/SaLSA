# Related research

## Raviv Group foundations

- [Szekely et al. (2011): ions and zwitterionic lipids](https://doi.org/10.1021/la200264s). Direct conceptual foundation. includes calcium partitioning measurements, unlike a spacing-only comparison.
- [Lotan et al. (2016): calcium adsorption conditions](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.6b02708). DLPC/Ca preparation, time and temperature context; its thresholds are not transferred to this series.
- [Fink et al. (2017): osmotic-stress-induced desorption](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.langmuir.7b00596). Controlled pressure/distance comparisons constrain charging beyond spacing versus salt alone.
- [Fink et al. (2020): calcium bicarbonate bridges](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.langmuir.0c01511). Different lipid/solution system; motivates attention to composition without proving bridging here.
- [Ben-Nun et al. (2010): X+](https://doi.org/10.1107/S0021889810032772) and [Szekely et al. (2010): form factors](https://doi.org/10.1021/la101433t). Original modeling lineage, not a claim that archived fits were regenerated.
- [Nadler et al. (2011): in-house SAXS setup](https://doi.org/10.1039/C0SM00824A). No import of its settings into the 2017 runs.
- [Ginsburg et al. (2019): D+](https://doi.org/10.1107/S1600576718018046). Roee Tekoah is a coauthor. A separate published software contribution; this repository does not claim to have migrated X+ fits to D+.

The group's [lipid](https://uriraviv.huji.ac.il/publications-subject/lipids), [membrane](https://uriraviv.huji.ac.il/publications-subject/membranes) and [software](https://uriraviv.huji.ac.il/publications-subject/software-and-algorithms) bibliographies provide broader context.
