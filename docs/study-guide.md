# Study guide

## The question

DPPC is a saturated phosphatidylcholine lipid. Its headgroup is zwitterionic, but ions may associate with the membrane and change its structure and interactions. The project compares five chloride-salt environments at nominally fixed preparation concentration. The recorded salt axis describes added salt, not measured free-metal concentration or matched ionic strength.

## From experiment to observation

The historical protocol prepared ethanol aliquots, lyophilized the lipid, hydrated it with salt solution, applied freeze–thaw treatment for the main protocol, equilibrated, transferred dispersions into capillaries and centrifuged them before SAXS. Some records belong to a separate No Cycling section or later centrifugation treatment. Fixed preparation concentration does not establish the local concentration at the beam after centrifugation.

SAXS intensity contains a bilayer envelope and correlations between bilayers. Multiple harmonically related peaks can support a lamellar repeat distance through `q_h = 2πh/D`. D includes the bilayer and intervening aqueous region; it is not the water gap alone. A single feature requires an order assignment, while absent peaks do not give an infinite D.

## The concentration series

The five concentration series remain the scientific core. Finite recorded spacings, ambiguous single-peak assignments, fit alternatives and missing statuses are displayed honestly. Traced manganese and nickel curves show contrasting envelopes/peaks and the limitations of their archived models. Copper 1 mM peak records support finite repeat distances near 6.34 nm. A cobalt-labeled export is excluded because its numerical source is a copper curve.

## Background sensitivity

For Ni RE45, illustrative ±1% and ±5% background-factor changes test the positions of two selected local intensity maxima. The resulting two-order spacing estimates span about 8.55858–8.55891 nm. The stored profile-fit centers instead reproduce the historical 8.520042 nm value. The difference is between estimators; local peak stability does not validate a full physical model or supply experimental error bars.

## The interpretation

The manuscript interprets the concentration dependence in terms of ion adsorption, membrane charging, adsorption saturation and electrolyte screening. Water-like repeat distances at low concentrations suggest weak adsorption; operational boundaries C1 and C2 delimit the swelling regime. Comparative swelling and screening behavior supports the qualitative apparent-affinity order Ni²⁺ < Co²⁺ < Fe³⁺ < Mn²⁺ < Cu²⁺. These are physical interpretations of SAXS structure, rather than direct measurements of bound-ion populations or binding constants. Temperature, solution chemistry, processing and preparation relationships constrain the comparison.
