# Authors and contributions

The authors, in order, are Roee Tekoah, Eitan Eisenthal Berkovitz, Lea Fink, Asaf Shemesh and Uri Raviv.

The research was conducted in the Raviv Group at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem in 2017–2018. Roee Tekoah performed sample preparation, SAXS measurements, integration and background subtraction, X+ fitting, figure preparation, interpretation and manuscript preparation.

The scattering workflow used FIT2D for integration and X+ for structural analysis. Relevant method publications are cited in the manuscript and the [research context](research-context.md).
